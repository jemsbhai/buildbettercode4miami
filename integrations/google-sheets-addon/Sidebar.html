USD positive donations only. Gift ID, name, amount and date are required. Use stable transaction IDs from your donation platform. Names are never identity keys.

Supported data & corrections

Up to 1,000 rows, 32 columns and 1 MB of selected data. Dates use YYYY-MM-DD or native date cells. Refunds, negative amounts and other currencies are not supported. Blank rows are skipped. Donor ID and channel are optional; missing donor IDs stay separate until you review matches. Every import replaces your previous table snapshot.
